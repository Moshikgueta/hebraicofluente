Privacy Policy

What data Hebraico Fluente collects, what it is for, who it is shared with and how you exercise your rights. In plain English.

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller of your data is the company identified at the end of this page, based in Brazil. Any request about personal data can be made by email, at the address in section 6. That address is the contact point for everything about your data, and it is answered within 15 days.

The legal bases are: the performance of the contract you made with us (art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) for everything about your access and progress; legal obligations for the tax data of the order and for the access log; our legitimate interests (art. 6(1)(f)) in keeping the service secure, protecting the course against copying and keeping backups; and your consent (art. 6(1)(a)) where the table says so.

Backups. Once a day we make a copy of the database, stored privately at Cloudflare and, encrypted (unreadable without our password), also on Google Drive, to restore the service if something goes wrong. Each copy is deleted automatically after 90 days. If you delete your account, your data leaves the system at once and leaves the backups when they expire; until then, backups are only used to restore the service, never for anything else.

Recording your voice. In the lessons, in the reading texts and in the demonstration on the home page (even without an account) you can record yourself reading, to hear yourself. The browser asks for permission to use the microphone, and the recording stays on your device only: it disappears when you leave the page and is never sent to us or anyone else.

Pronunciation check. If you use “Speak and get feedback”, in the lessons or in the demonstration on the home page, your speech is transcribed by your browser's speech recognition service - Google's, in Chrome and on Android, or Apple's, in Safari and on the iPhone - under the privacy rules of whoever made the browser. The course does not receive your audio: it receives only the transcribed text, compares it with the word on screen and does not keep it. If you prefer your voice not to leave your device, use only “Record mine”.

3. Who we share it with

As little as possible, and never to sell data - we do not sell, rent or give away personal data to anyone.

  • ·Paddle: our online reseller and the Merchant of Record of every purchase. When you pay, we pass your email to Paddle's checkout, and Paddle collects what it needs for the payment and for tax (such as your card or PayPal details, country and postal code) directly. Paddle processes that data as an independent controller, under its own privacy policy. We never receive or store your card details.
  • ·Resend: delivers our emails (password reset, purchase receipt, up to three reminders to start the course in the first days after buying, and notices about your account). For that it receives your email address and the name on your account. Promotional emails only go to people who asked for them, and each one carries a link to leave the list.
  • ·Infrastructure: the platform runs on Cloudflare, which hosts the site, the database and the backups. A second, encrypted backup is kept on Google Drive; Google stores the file but cannot read it.
  • ·GitHub: stores the site's code and our operations tools. When we look up or change an account's access (for example, to grant a course or fix an email), the operation runs there, and its record shows the email partly hidden.
  • ·Support: if you write to us, the message stays in the support inbox (Gmail, from Google) or on WhatsApp (from Meta). Purchase and cancellation notices reach the same inbox, with your email.
  • ·Authorities, when there is a legal order to do so.

The company is in Brazil, and Paddle, Resend, Cloudflare, GitHub, Google and Meta are companies based in other countries, so part of the processing happens on servers outside the country where you live, including the United States. These transfers are necessary to perform the contract you made with us - they are what lets the site respond, the payment happen, the email arrive and support answer.

4. Cookies and measurement

We use one session cookie, needed to keep you signed in. It is signed, short-lived and cannot be used to track your browsing elsewhere.

Your browser also keeps your progress locally, so the course works offline, and some of your preferences (such as the accessibility settings). That stays on your device only. We use no advertising cookies, no ad network and no third-party script to measure audience. The Paddle checkout, which only loads on the payment page, is Paddle's and follows Paddle's privacy policy.

To improve the lessons, people signed in to the course also send, in batches, learning counts only: what type of exercise, from which lesson, whether the answer was right or wrong, and which step of which lesson was completed for the first time, with the time range it took (for example, “between 2 and 4 minutes”, counting only the time the page was visible and in use; never the exact time). The server adds these numbers up by day and does not keep name, email, account, IP address, time of day or what you typed. The totals are deleted after a year.

In the free demo on the course page, the browser sends only the name of each step you reached, once per visit, so we know where people stop. On the first page you open, the browser notes where you came from, in a closed list of categories (such as “search”, “email” or “ad”) and, if the link has one, the campaign name; that note stays on your device for 30 days, without a cookie, and reaches the server only as a daily total and, if you buy, on the order, so we know which channels bring students. The server keeps no name, email, account, IP address, time of day or exact site you came from for any of these counts. The totals are deleted after a year.

At the end of the demo you can ask for the second letter by email. If you do not tick the box for the next letters, the address you type is used only to send that email and is not kept or added to any list. So as not to send twice on the same day, the server keeps for up to 48 hours a code calculated from the address, which cannot be turned back into it.

The interest list. If you tick the box for the next letters at the end of the demo, or ask to be told when a course opens, we keep your email, what you asked for, the category you came from and the dates of sign-up. The legal basis is your consent. Your sign-up only counts after you confirm it through the link in the first email; without confirmation nothing else is sent and the address is deleted in 7 days. Every email on the list carries a link to leave, which works without signing in; when you leave, your email is deleted from the list and we send nothing more.

5. Security

  • ·Passwords stored as a salted hash (PBKDF2), never in readable form.
  • ·All traffic over HTTPS.
  • ·HttpOnly session cookie - the page's JavaScript cannot read it.
  • ·Attempt limiter on sign-in, against automated password guessing.
  • ·Database access restricted to the application.
  • ·Daily private backup, with restoration tested.

No system is perfect. If a security incident creates a relevant risk to your data, we will tell you and the competent authorities, as the law requires.

6. Your rights, and how to use them

Depending on where you live (in the European Union and the United Kingdom, under the GDPR), you have the right to:

  • ·know whether we process your data, and access it;
  • ·correct data that is incomplete, inaccurate or out of date;
  • ·have your data deleted;
  • ·restrict how your data is processed;
  • ·receive your data in a portable format;
  • ·object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • ·withdraw your consent at any time, where processing is based on it;
  • ·complain to the data protection authority of the country where you live.

California residents: we do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and you have the rights to know, delete and correct it, and not to be treated differently for using them.

Where to send requests about personal data

Write to languageswithmoshik@gmail.com with the subject Data protection request, from the email on your account. We reply within 15 days.

We ask that the request come from the account's email because that is how we confirm you are you - acting on a deletion request from someone else would be exactly the problem this policy exists to prevent.

You can download a copy of your data yourself, on your account page, under Download my data: you get a file with your sign-up details, purchases, acceptances, access logs and progress. For security, the password and the session and device identifiers are not in the file.

You can delete your account yourself, at any time, on your account page. We ask for your password at that moment - that is what makes sure the person deleting is you - and the deletion takes effect immediately, without waiting for anyone. Erasing only your progress, keeping the account, is also up to you, on the achievements page.

What deletion removes: email, name, password, progress and access to the course. What it does not remove, because the law does not allow it: the record of your purchases, which stays without your name and without your email, because keeping sales records is an obligation of the seller. What remains is the amount, date and status of the payment, and it does not point to you. For the same reason the access logs from the last six months stay until they reach the period the law sets, and are then deleted.

The email address above still works for everything else - access, correction, portability - and also if you prefer us to do the deletion for you.

7. Children and teenagers

The platform is made for adults. We do not direct content to people under 18 or knowingly collect their data. If we find an account created by a minor without their parents' or guardians' consent, it will be removed.

8. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. A relevant change in how we process your data is announced by email before it takes effect.
