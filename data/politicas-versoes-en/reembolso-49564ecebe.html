Refund and Cancellation Policy

You have 14 days to cancel your purchase and get everything back, without having to explain why.

Last updated: October 29, 2026

The short version

Bought it and changed your mind? On Your account, use Cancel the purchase, or write to languageswithmoshik@gmail.com within 14 days. We refund 100% of the amount, with no fee and no questions. It does not matter how many lessons you opened.

1. Your right to cancel

Every purchase gives you 14 calendar days to cancel, counted from when access is granted - the day the course opens for you. The right has no conditions.

In particular, we do not ask for a reason, we do not ask you to prove anything and we do not charge a cancellation fee. Having watched lessons does not take the right away. If the consumer law of your country gives you more than this, that applies too.

2. How to ask

On the site, in a minute: go to Your account, find the purchase under Purchases and use Cancel the purchase. The request is recorded right away, with the date and time, and the confirmation reaches your email at the same moment. You do not need to do anything else.

If you prefer, by email:

  • ·Write to languageswithmoshik@gmail.com from the same email you used to buy.
  • ·Put Refund request in the subject. Nothing else is needed - not even an order number; we find it from your email.

On the site, the confirmation reaches your e-mail right away. By e-mail, we reply confirming receipt within 2 business days, and what counts is the date you wrote, not the date of our reply.

Asking from the email you used to buy is the only requirement, and it exists to protect you: it is how we confirm that the person asking for the refund is the person who paid.

3. When the money comes back

The refund is requested from Paddle, our reseller and the Merchant of Record of the purchase, right away. It goes back to the payment method you used (card, PayPal, Apple Pay or Google Pay), for the full amount. When it shows up on your statement depends on your bank or card issuer, not on us.

4. What happens to your access

Access to the course ends when the refund is processed - that is the counterpart of getting your money back. Your study progress is not erased: if you buy again one day, it will be there.

5. After the 14 days

After the cancellation period, the purchase gives you the 12 months of access you bought and, as a rule, is not refundable.

There are two situations in which we refund anyway, with no time limit: a technical problem that keeps you from using the course and that we cannot solve, and an incorrect charge - a duplicate, an amount different from the one shown, or a purchase you did not make. In both cases, write to us. We will sort it out.

6. Cancelling is not the same as getting a refund

There is no subscription and no recurring charge here: you pay once for the period you bought, and nothing is charged again automatically. So there is nothing to cancel to “stop being charged”.

If you only want to close your account and leave, you do that yourself, on the Your account page, at any time. It is permanent, and it takes your progress and your access to the course with it - with no refund. If you are still within the 14 days and want your money back, use Cancel the purchase first.
